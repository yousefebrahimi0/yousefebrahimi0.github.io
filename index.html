About me

The bridge between "we have data" and "now we know what to do with it." I sit at the intersection of Advanced Analytics, Digital Growth, and Product Architecture. While many either get buried purely in code or float in high-level marketing theory, I operate across both worlds: engineering robust Python data pipelines to extract reliable signals, and executing technical strategies (CRO, predictive modeling, and technical SEO) that convert those signals into measurable growth. Over the past 8+ years across enterprise leaders like Cisco, high-growth academic hubs like Nova SBE, and luxury global firms like Nest Seekers, I have specialized in turning fragmented data streams into automated pipelines and clear executive roadmaps. In addition to enterprise work, I build 0-to-1 products as the Founder of GreenRah, a climate-tech urban navigation app helping pedestrians navigate European cities through shaded, cooler routes.

My Approach:

I treat business challenges like an investigation and data as the evidence. Ingest & Clean: I build automated Python pipelines and data cleansing routines to ensure the baseline evidence is 100% sound. Model & Discover: Using predictive modeling and cohort analysis, I uncover patterns—identifying churn risks, lifecycle opportunities, or traffic bottlenecks. Visualize & Align: I build interactive Tableau, Power BI, and Looker Studio dashboards that translate complex telemetry into intuitive stories for stakeholders. Test & Scale: Through rigorous multivariate A/B testing, GA4 funnel instrumentation, and AI-accelerated workflows, I close the loop from insight to measurable growth.
